## Description

Operationally deployed phenotype identifying Non-ST-elevation myocardial infarction (NSTEMI) admissions across Advocate Health and Atrium Health network sites. Supports the NSTEMI quality measure reporting program aligned to ACC/AHA chest-pain guidelines (2021) and CMS-based quality reporting. Entry criterion: OMOP concept 314666, mapped from ICD-10 I21.4. Tightened v0.2.0+ definition adds high-sensitivity cardiac troponin I >2x ULN within 24 hours of the index encounter.


## Inclusion Criteria

1. Condition occurrence: OMOP concept 314666 (NSTEMI), mapped from ICD-10 I21.4;
   includeDescendants = true, includeMapped = true.
2. Encounter setting: inpatient or emergency department visit
   (condition_type_concept_id IN 32817, 32020).
3. Tightened definition (v0.2.0+): high-sensitivity cardiac troponin I
   (LOINC 10839-9, OMOP 3016502) > 2x upper limit of normal (ULN = 34 ng/L;
   threshold = 68 ng/L) within 24 hours of index NSTEMI date.
4. Index event on or after 2020-01-01 (EHR data completeness threshold).


## Exclusion Criteria

1. Concurrent STEMI diagnosis (ICD-10 I21.0-I21.3, OMOP 312327) within ±1 day
   of index event — managed on a separate quality pathway.
2. Type 2 MI (demand ischemia, ICD-10 I21.A1) documented by provider narrative
   or clinical clarification flag.
3. Insufficient data for troponin assessment (v0.2.0+ only).
