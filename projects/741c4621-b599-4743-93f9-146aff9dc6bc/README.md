## Description

Identifies Non-ST-elevation myocardial infarction (NSTEMI) admissions across the Advocate Health and Atrium Health network for quality measure reporting aligned to ACC/AHA chest-pain guidelines (2021) and CMS-based quality reporting.

## Inclusion Criteria

1. Condition occurrence: OMOP concept 314666 (NSTEMI), ICD-10 I21.4; includeDescendants = true, includeMapped = true.
2. Encounter setting: inpatient or emergency department visit (condition_type_concept_id IN 32817, 32020).
3. High-sensitivity cardiac troponin I (LOINC 10839-9, OMOP 3016502) > 2× ULN (threshold 68 ng/L, ULN = 34 ng/L) within 24h of index date.

1. Concurrent STEMI (ICD-10 I21.0–I21.3, OMOP 312327) within ±1 day of index event.
2. Type 2 MI (demand ischemia, ICD-10 I21.A1) by provider documentation.
